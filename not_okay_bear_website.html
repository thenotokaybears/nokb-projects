FAQ

Is this actually therapy?
Nope. Not Okay Bear is an AI. If you're actually struggling with mental health stuff, see a real therapist or call a crisis line. Not Okay Bear knows its limits.
Why does it get sarcastic?
Because people grow through honesty, not coddling. Early messages are supportive. Later messages are real. That arc mirrors actual growth.
Will my conversations be stored?
Not by default. Your chats are yours. We're not selling your data or analyzing it for ads. You just talk to a bear and move on.
Can the bear actually help me?
Can a bear actually help? Nah. But it can help you help yourself. It'll ask the right questions, call you on your BS, and remind you that you're not alone. That's something.
Is this just for NOKB holders?
Built by NOKB, for NOKB. But honestly? If you get the philosophy, you're part of the community. Come hang. 🐻
