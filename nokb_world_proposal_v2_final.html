Compliance & Safety Requirements
  • Child Protection License: Required for any facility serving children. 4-6 weeks via MENJE (Ministry of Education). Annual renewal.
  • Safety Standards: EU EN 1176/1177 (playground equipment). Annual inspections mandatory.
  • Staff-to-Child Ratio: 1:15 standard (Luxembourg). NOKB budgets 1:12 for premium experience.
  • Food Safety: HACCP certification (€3-5k) + staff handler training (ongoing).
  • Insurance: Public liability (required), property, staff training documentation.
  • Building Code: Standard commercial occupancy + child facility add-ons (egress, safety zones, etc.).
