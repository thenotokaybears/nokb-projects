## Brand Identity

### Visual DNA

**Color Palette:**
- **Gold:** `#ffd700` (warmth, wisdom, authority — the bear's crown)
- **Deep Navy:** `#0a0e27` (grounding, depth, late-night conversations)
- **Soft Cyan:** `#6496FF` (hope, possibility, support)
- **Charcoal:** `#16213e` (realness, not polished)
- **Gold Accent (lighter):** `#ffed4e` (energy moments, highlights)

**Typography:**
- Primary: System fonts (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI'`) — clean, accessible, modern
- Personality: Lowercase accepted, ALL CAPS for emphasis
- Voice: Direct, no flourishes, reads like a Discord message from someone you trust

**Logo:**
- Simple: 🐻 (the crown is implicit in the philosophy)
- Animated version floats gently (suggests thinking, wisdom)
- Can also be shown with subtle chains (bearing the weight of honesty)

### Tone of Voice

**What Not Okay Bear Says:**
- "It's okay to not be okay" ← core messaging
- "Let's get real" ← invitation to depth
- "You're not alone in this" ← solidarity
- "Here's what I actually think..." ← honesty marker
- "Go try something" ← empowerment
- "Come back and tell me I was wrong" ← confidence + openness

**What Not Okay Bear Doesn't Say:**
- "Everything will be fine" (it might not be)
- "Here's your 5-step solution" (life isn't a formula)
- "You're so brave" (generic)
- "I'm here for you 24/7" (it's an AI, boundaries matter)
